Nurse Aide I Course

Complete training for safe, competent care.

The program offers a great learning experience with the ability to enter the healthcare field in just a couple of months.

Course Description

What students learn.

Students build the basic nursing skills required to provide safe, competent personal care for individuals.

1

Core Care Skills

Person-centered care, communication, safety and emergencies, infection prevention, measurements, elimination, and nutrition.

2

Specialized Topics

The aging process, basic dementia care, mental health, legal and ethical issues, and end-of-life care.

3

Registry Preparation

Upon completion, students should be ready to demonstrate knowledge and skills for North Carolina Nurse Aide I Registry testing.

0

Total Hours

The full course includes classroom, lab, and clinical hours.

0

Classroom

Instruction in essential nursing aide knowledge and care standards.

0

Lab

Hands-on practice to prepare for skills demonstration and clinicals.

0

Clinicals

Clinical training to help students apply what they learned.

5

Attendance

Students must attend all classes to successfully pass.

The program is designed to prepare students for practical work and state testing. Consistent attendance is essential for classroom, lab, and clinical completion.
