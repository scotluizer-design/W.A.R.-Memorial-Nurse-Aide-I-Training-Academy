Our Mission

Helping students prepare for certification and purpose.

Our mission is to help as many in-home aides as possible prepare to take the test and get their state certification. The academy also welcomes anyone with a passion to care for the sick, frail, mentally challenged, and those who need compassionate support.

  • Prepare students for Nurse Aide I Registry testing
  • Build confidence through classroom, lab, and clinical practice
  • Inspire students to pursue nursing and further medical careers
Goal

Supply the job market with quality employees ready to apply their training in the field.

Founder Legacy

Honoring Dr. W. A. Reeves.

W.A.R. stands for William Alphonza Reeves Memorial. The late Dr. W. A. Reeves, Mrs. Tankard's father, was the first African-American male L.P.N. in North Carolina.

He contributed to the Durham community as a minister, business man, innovator, mentor, friend, and supporter of good causes. The academy carries forward that legacy of service.
