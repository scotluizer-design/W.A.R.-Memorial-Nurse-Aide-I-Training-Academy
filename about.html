Our Story

Honoring William Alphonza Reeves.

W.A.R. stands for William Alphonza Reeves Memorial. The late Dr. W. A. Reeves, Mrs. Tankard's father, was the first African-American male L.P.N. in North Carolina.

In the late 1950s and early 1960s, Hillside High School had a special one-year program to help recent African-American graduates become Licensed Practical Nurses. Dr. Reeves enrolled, and that opportunity changed his family forever.

He served the Durham community as a minister, business man, innovator, mentor, friend, and supporter of good causes.

Our Mission

Prepare students to take the test and earn state certification.

Our mission is to help as many in-home aides as possible prepare to take the test and get their state certification. Training is not limited to in-home aides; it is available to all who want to enter the medical field and go the extra mile.

Goal

Quality employees for the field

Our goal is to supply the job market with quality employees who are ready to apply what they learned in the field and pursue higher medical goals.
